Conclusion

This project set out to explore the lyrics of A Flair for the Dramatic and Selfish Machines through text-based analysis. As someone who has been a fan of Pierce The Veil for many years, I was interested in seeing whether data analysis would reveal patterns that are not immediately noticeable when simply listening to the music. By using both Voyant Tools and AntConc, I was able to examine the lyrics from multiple perspectives and gain a deeper understanding of the language used throughout the band's early work.

The results from Voyant Tools showed that both albums contain a strong emphasis on emotionally charged language. Words related to relationships, personal experiences, and internal struggles appeared frequently throughout the text clouds. Although the two albums share several common themes, the analysis also revealed differences in vocabulary between them. Certain words appeared much more prominently in one album than the other, suggesting that while the albums explore similar subject matter, they often do so using different language and lyrical approaches. The frequency graphs further demonstrated that these words are not distributed evenly throughout each album. Instead, many terms appear in clusters or spikes, indicating that particular songs contribute heavily to overall word frequency.

AntConc expanded upon these findings by providing additional context for how words and phrases were actually used within the lyrics. The concordance analysis made it possible to examine recurring words in their surrounding context rather than viewing them as isolated data points. This provided a more detailed look at the language patterns present within each album. The phrase analysis also revealed how often repeated lyrical segments appear in the songs. Many of the most common three-word and six-word phrases originated from choruses, refrains, and other repeated passages. These repetitions play an important role in shaping the overall vocabulary of each album and help explain why certain words and phrases appear so frequently in the data.

One of the most interesting aspects of this project was seeing how the two tools complemented one another. Voyant Tools provided broad visualizations that made large-scale patterns easy to identify, while AntConc allowed those patterns to be examined in greater detail. Together, they transformed hundreds of lines of song lyrics into measurable data that could be compared, explored, and interpreted.

Overall, this analysis demonstrates that text-analysis software can offer valuable insights into music and songwriting. While statistics and frequency counts cannot fully capture the meaning or emotional impact of a song, they can reveal trends that might otherwise go unnoticed. By combining quantitative analysis with close observation, it becomes possible to gain a deeper appreciation for the language, structure, and recurring themes found within Pierce The Veil's lyrics.
