These are lots of things I love!

Generally speaking, I love my family, my pets, my boyfriend, art, and video games. Quite the extensive list!

My immediate family consists of my mom and my dad. They're my biggest supporters! While in school, my dad and I try to get a meal together as often as we can. My mom is basically my built-in best friend- we call nearly every day!

Pets

I have a cat named Karma and a dog named Ivy. Karma was a stray; he was estimated to be about 2-3 when we found him, making him about 14 now. Although he's a senior, he still has the energy of a kitten- he's such a lovebug! We got Ivy when she was a puppy. She's turning 11 on October 10th, 2026- say happy birthday to her! She struggles with some health problems now that she's older, but she's still the sweetest and smartest dog around. She's a certified AKC Trick Dog (albeit, I don't remember her exact title).

Boyfriend

My boyfriend's name is Brian. We went to the same high school, and we've been dating for nearly 2.5 years! For the sake of not getting too sappy... he's my best friend, he's amazing, and I am so grateful to have him in my life :)

Art

I love many forms of art. Over the past few years, I've spent lots of time crocheting, drawing, painting, and doing video editing. Even though I don't have much free time to engage in those hobbies, I get a lot of that same fulfillment out of being a DIGIT major!

Video Games

I've spent countless hours playing video games since I was a kid. I grew up playing lots of Nintendo games, and I still do to this day- it's very nostalgic for me! I've been loving nostalgic games and technology, so much so that I own and regularly use a 3DS, an iPod classic, and a retro game emulator (Dolphin on PC). I've figured out how to reliably run Super Mario Galaxy on my PC, and it's been really fun. Other than that, I really love games within the The Legend of Zelda franchise.
